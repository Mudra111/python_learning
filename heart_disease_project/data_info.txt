Age : Age of the patient (in years)
Sex : M → Male , F → Female (Males generally have higher heart disease risk (statistically))
ChestPainType : TA (Typical Angina) -> Classic heart pain (due to blocked arteries)
                ATA (Atypical Angina) -> Not typical, but still may indicate heart issue
                NAP (Non-Anginal Pain) -> Chest pain NOT related to heart
                ASY (Asymptomatic) -> No chest pain at all (dangerous because silent)
RestingBP (Resting Blood Pressure) : Blood pressure when patient is at rest, Unit: mm Hg
                                     High BP = higher risk
Cholesterol : Cholesterol level in blood , Unit: mg/dL
              High cholesterol = fat buildup in arteries → heart risk
FastingBS (Fasting Blood Sugar) : 1 → >120 mg/dL (High), 0 → Normal
                                  High sugar may indicate diabetes → increases heart risk
RestingECG (Electrocardiogram) : Measures electrical activity of heart , Helps detect heart abnormalities
                                 Normal
                                 ST → abnormal heart activity
                                 LVH (Left Ventricular Hypertrophy) → thickened heart wall (bad sign) 
MaxHR (Maximum Heart Rate) : Max heart rate achieved during exercise , Lower-than-expected values may indicate heart issues
ExerciseAngina : Y → Yes (pain during exercise) , N → No (Pain during exercise = strong indicator of heart disease)
Oldpeak : ST depression (difference in ECG before & after exercise) , Higher value = more abnormal heart response
ST_Slope : Slope of ECG signal during exercise
           Up → Normal , Flat → Risk , Down → High risk
HeartDisease (target) : 0 → No heart disease , 1 → Has heart disease
